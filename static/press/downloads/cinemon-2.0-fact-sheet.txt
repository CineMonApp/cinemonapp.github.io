CINEMON 2.0 | PRODUCT FACT SHEET
October 20, 2026

CineMon is a professional video and audio monitoring app for iPad and Mac. It combines scopes and video assist tools, multi-source audio mixing, and recording with slate metadata and timecode.

Product
CineMon 2.0

Launch
October 20, 2026

Platforms
USB-C iPad with iPadOS 26 or later; Mac with macOS Tahoe 26 or later.

Developer
CineSpices, LLC. Created by Ike Arias.

Video
Two simultaneous live video sources, Reference Mode, video scopes, composition and focus tools, LUTs, and input color management.

Audio
Multi-source mixing, six audio scopes, and ITU-R BS.1770 loudness metering.

Recording
ProRes, H.264, and 10-bit HEVC on supported encoders, with digital slates, metadata, timecode, and a Media Library.

Video input hardware
USB capture devices on iPad and Mac; Accsoon SeeMo on iPad; Blackmagic Design DeckLink and UltraStudio on Mac.

Availability
Free download on the App Store, with one-time PRO Packs and a PRO Pass subscription.
