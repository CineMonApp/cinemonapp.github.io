## Feature access

The **Histogram** is always free. The **Waveform Monitor** and **Vectorscope** are available with the CineMon **PRO Video Monitoring Pack** or a CineMon **PRO Pass** subscription.

## Crop to Zoomed Image

When you zoom into the monitor, you can choose whether the scopes measure the whole source image or only the part you currently see. Turn on **Crop to Zoomed Image** in the [Scope Settings](/docs/monitoring/scope-settings/#scope-settings) popover to analyze just the visible region, which is useful for checking exposure or color in a specific area of the frame.

Like the audio scopes, you can assign a video scope to a [Specialized Monitor](/docs/start-here/interface/#specialized-monitors) or open it as a [Floating Window](/docs/start-here/interface/#floating-scopes).
