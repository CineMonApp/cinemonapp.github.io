**Display LUT** applies a lookup table to the monitor image. The tool is labeled **LUT** in CineMon. Use it to preview a log-to-display transform or a creative look. Import and manage your LUTs in the [LUTs organizer](/docs/customize/organizers/luts/), and see [Color management](/docs/monitoring/video/color-management/) for how the LUT fits into the processing chain.

Available with the CineMon **PRO Video Monitoring Pack** or a CineMon **PRO Pass** subscription.

A **show LUT** previews a production’s intended grade while you monitor the camera image.

## Basic use

Long-press the **LUT** button in the **Assist Toolbar** to open or close its options drawer. On Mac, you can also select the chevron beside the button. Select the tool button to turn the effect on or off.

1. Choose an imported LUT from **LUT Menu**.
2. If you have several LUTs, use **Previous / Next** to compare their effect on the image.
