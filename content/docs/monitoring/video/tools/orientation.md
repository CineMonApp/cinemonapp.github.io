**Orientation** flips the image horizontally or vertically, or rotates it 90 degrees clockwise or counterclockwise. Use it for a camera mounted upside down or sideways, or when filming through a mirror.

Available with the CineMon **PRO Video Monitoring Pack** or a CineMon **PRO Pass** subscription.

{{< screenshot name="orientation" alt="Orientation Tool"  caption="Correct the preview orientation for a camera mounted sideways or upside down." >}}

## Basic use

Long-press the **Orientation** button in the **Assist Toolbar** to open or close its options drawer. On Mac, you can also select the chevron beside the button. Select the tool button to turn the effect on or off.

1. Choose **Flip Vertical** or **Flip Horizontal** to correct a mirrored or inverted image.
2. For a sideways camera, turn on **Rotate 90°** and choose the rotation direction.

## Options

{{< screenshot name="orientation-options" alt="Orientation options drawer with flip and rotation controls" max-width="555px" >}}

| Option | Values | Default | Description |
|--------|-----------|---------|--------|
| Flip Vertical | `On`/`Off` | `On` | Flips the image vertically. |
| Flip Horizontal | `On`/`Off` | `Off` | Flips the image horizontally. |
| Rotate 90° | `On`/`Off` | `Off` | Rotates the image 90 degrees. |
| Rotation Direction | `Left`/`Right` | `Left` | Determines if the image is rotated left (counterclockwise) or right (clockwise). |
