The **Audio Scopes** display levels, loudness, stereo balance, phase, and frequency content for the audio you hear. For source setup and mixing, see [Audio Monitoring](/docs/monitoring/audio/) and the [Audio Mixer](/docs/monitoring/audio/mixer/).

## Feature access

The **Levels** meter is free to use: peak bars, the scale, and the readouts need no purchase. Its RMS bars and history graph, and all of the other audio scopes, are available with the CineMon **PRO Audio Monitoring Pack** or a CineMon **PRO Pass** subscription.

Like the video scopes, you can assign an audio scope to a [Specialized Monitor](/docs/start-here/interface/#specialized-monitors) or open it as a [Floating Window](/docs/start-here/interface/#floating-scopes).
