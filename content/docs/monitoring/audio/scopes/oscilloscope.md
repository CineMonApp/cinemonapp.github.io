The **Oscilloscope** draws the audio waveform over time, including amplitude changes, transients, and gaps. Use it to spot clipping, asymmetry, or unexpected DC offset.

{{< screenshot name="oscilloscope" alt="Stereo audio waveforms in separate oscilloscope panes" caption="The two panes show the left and right channel waveforms." >}}

When the input falls silent, the trace settles to a flat line instead of holding the last waveform.

You can show or hide the amplitude labels on the display.

## Basic use

Open **Scopes** in the status toolbar and select **Oscilloscope**. If the menu is hidden in a narrow window, open **More Controls** (**…**).

Use the scope’s settings button to adjust the options described below.

1. Play or monitor the source and inspect the waveform for sudden changes or a flattened shape.
2. Turn on **Labels** when you need amplitude references.

## Options

| Setting | Options | Description |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| Labels | On / Off | Show the amplitude labels on the display. |

The Oscilloscope is available with the CineMon **PRO Audio Monitoring Pack** or a CineMon **PRO Pass** subscription.
