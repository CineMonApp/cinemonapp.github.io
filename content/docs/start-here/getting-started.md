This guide takes you through one monitoring session: open a source, display its picture, and turn on composition guides. You can use a video file if you don't have a camera connected. These steps use free features.

## 1. Install and open CineMon

| Device | Requirements |
|--------|--------------|
| iPad | USB-C iPad running iPadOS 26 or later. Lightning models are not supported. |
| Mac | macOS Tahoe 26 or later. An Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later) is recommended. |

[Download CineMon from the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cinemon/id6504142777). Open the app and select **Continue** on the welcome screen. The **Home** screen is where you add and configure sources.

{{< screenshot name="first-time-welcome" alt="Welcome to CineMon 2.0 with a feature overview and Continue button" caption="Select Continue to open Home and add your first source." max-width="560px" >}}

## 2. Add a source

Choose one of these routes:

### Use a video file

1. On **Home**, select **Add Source** (**+**).
2. Choose **Media File**, then select a video on your device and add it.
3. The file appears as a source card on Home.

### Connect a camera

1. Connect the camera's HDMI or SDI output to a compatible video capture adapter, then connect the adapter to your iPad or Mac. Some cameras can send video directly over USB.
2. On **Home**, select **Add Source** (**+**) and choose **Camera**.
3. Select your capture device and add it. Allow camera access when prompted.

> A capture adapter sends video **into** your iPad or Mac. A USB-to-HDMI display cable sends video out and cannot replace a capture adapter. See [Video input options](/docs/monitoring/video/sources/) for device types and connection details.

You can also choose a supported built-in camera. For an audio-only session, follow [Audio sources & devices](/docs/monitoring/audio/sources/).

## 3. Display the picture

Select **Monitor** (the TV icon) on the source card. Its button turns green and CineMon opens the **Monitor** screen. For a video file, use **Play** on the floating transport bar to start playback.

The video slots are named **V1** and **V2**. With one source, you only need V1. You can return to Home from the sidebar to adjust a source without stopping its picture.

{{< screenshot name="home-sources-workspace" alt="Video source card with its green Monitor button at the upper right" caption="The TV icon opens Monitor. Green indicates that the source is already on the monitor." max-width="360px" >}}

If you don't see a picture, follow [No picture](/docs/help/troubleshooting/#no-picture-on-the-monitor).

## 4. Try a composition guide

1. In the **Assist Toolbar** (at the bottom by default), select **Composition Guides**.
2. Long-press the tool button to open its options drawer. On Mac, press and hold the mouse or trackpad button; you can also select the small chevron.
3. Turn on **Grid**. Set **Vertical** and **Horizontal** to `2` for a rule-of-thirds grid.
4. Adjust the guide color or line width to suit the picture. Long-press the tool button again to close the drawer.

The guide helps you judge framing while you monitor. See [Composition Guides](/docs/monitoring/video/tools/composition-guides/) for other arrangements and options.

## 5. Find your way around

- **Home:** add sources and adjust their settings.
- **Monitor:** view the picture and use tools. Pinch with two fingers on the iPad screen or a Mac trackpad to zoom; drag to pan while zoomed.
- **Sidebar:** move between screens, open settings, or select **Help** to open the documentation. Use the sidebar button or `⌃⌘S` with a hardware keyboard.

For the full toolbar map, gestures, and keyboard shortcuts, see [User interface](/docs/start-here/interface/).
