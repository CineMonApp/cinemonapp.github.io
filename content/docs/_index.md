## Reading this guide

Control names appear in **bold**. Instructions apply to both iPad and Mac unless marked {{< ipad >}} or {{< mac >}}. **Long-press** means touch and hold on iPad, or press and hold the mouse or trackpad button on Mac. Keyboard shortcuts require a hardware keyboard; pinch and swipe gestures on Mac use a trackpad. Screenshots illustrate the relevant tool; window size and platform can change the arrangement of controls.

A {{< pro-pill >}} label identifies a paid feature. Each feature page explains which pack unlocks it; [Packs & purchases](/docs/help/purchases/) compares the options.

This guide describes the latest public release. See [What's new](/docs/start-here/whats-new/) for highlights and [Release notes](/docs/help/release-notes/) for the full history. For help or corrections, email [support@cinemon.app](mailto:support@cinemon.app).
