Ecosystem

CineMon Industry Partners

CineMon works seamlessly with a variety of video input hardware that delivers UVC video over USB — HDMI- or SDI-to-USB capture adapters, wireless video receivers, and many modern cameras — enabling filmmakers to access industry-grade workflows across various budget tiers. Beyond UVC-compliant devices, CineMon also collaborates with leading hardware and software partners to support a broader range of video input formats and form factors.

Interested in becoming a CineMon partner? Contact us at partners@cinemon.app.

Accsoon SeeMo Devices

CineMon supports Accsoon SeeMo devices — compact and affordable solutions that turn iOS devices into on-camera monitors. CineMon can process video from a SeeMo, SeeMo 4K, or SeeMo Pro (with HDMI and SDI inputs). Combine CineMon’s professional-grade tools and customizable workflows with SeeMo’s integrated form factor for a compact yet powerful monitoring solution.
